About Healthy Leaf

A university project dedicated to making healthy eating simple and accessible for everyone.

Our Mission

Healthy Leaf was created to bridge the gap between knowing what to eat and actually eating it. As MIS students, we wanted to build a practical system that allows users to easily connect with diet experts and customize their daily meals without hassle.

The Team

We are a group of 4 MIS students passionate about using technology to solve real-world health management problems. This website demonstrates our ability to implement user authentication, dynamic data handling, and local storage for state management.
